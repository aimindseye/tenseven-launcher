# TenSeven Launcher 1.0.0

TenSeven Launcher 1.0.0 brings the BlackBerry-inspired home experience to Android 14 and newer.

## Download and install directly — no computer needed

The biggest change from the alpha releases: **the release APK supports Google Play Protect
scanning and can be installed directly after downloading it from GitHub.** You no longer
need ADB or a computer to install TenSeven.

1. On your phone, open the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/aimindseye/tenseven-launcher/releases).
2. Download `TenSeven-Launcher-1.0.0.apk` from the 1.0.0 release.
3. Open the downloaded APK. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install apps.
4. Keep Play Protect enabled and follow Android's installation and scanning prompts.
5. Open TenSeven and choose it as your default Home app.

## Your home, your way

- Choose a BlackBerry 7 or BlackBerry 10 home layout.
- Find apps quickly with keyboard search and letter shortcuts.
- Personalise themes, wallpapers, icon sizes and app grids.
- Choose the status dials you want, including the new storage dial.
- Show all notification-app icons on Home or use a compact view. Tap an icon to see that app's notifications in Hub.

## Stay on top of conversations

- Hub brings your selected messaging and email notifications together.
- Reply inline when supported, mark conversations read, snooze them or set a reminder.
- Reach calls, contacts, calendar, snoozed conversations and reminders from one navigation row.
  On smaller screens, swipe the row sideways to reach every shortcut.
- Peek at conversations from Home in a rounded panel that follows the launcher theme.

## Pick up where you left off

- BlackBerry 10 Active Frames keep the last eight apps you opened. Opening a ninth replaces the oldest.
- Available music, podcast, audiobook and calendar information appears inside the matching app's frame.
- Optional notification summaries help you catch up without opening an app.
- Add widgets to the Widgets page, reorder them and adjust their height.

## Useful extras

Enable the optional Notes, Tasks/Remind, Calc Plus, Sound Profiles and games.
Layouts adapt to the available screen space, with keyboard controls on supported devices
and touch navigation on ordinary Android phones.

## Permissions you control

Basic app launching and recent-app cards work without notification access. To use Hub and
media or notification summaries, enable notification access for TenSeven and choose which
apps appear in Hub. Calendar events need Calendar permission and calendar selection in
Hub settings.

If Android blocks notification access after installation, open **Settings → Apps →
TenSeven Launcher → three-dot menu → Allow restricted settings**, when offered, then
return to enable notification access. This is separate from installing the APK.

## Things to know

- Word, Excel and PowerPoint appear as recent-app cards; private document previews and file linking are not available.
- Media summaries depend on information the player provides, including whether it continues playing in the background.
- Screen sharing asks for Android's capture consent and provides a separate preview. Protected content may be excluded.
- Closing an Active Frame removes its card; it does not stop the app.
- Titan 2 has been tested by the owner. Further testing on other phones is still welcome.

See the [user guide](https://github.com/aimindseye/tenseven-launcher/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md)
for setup and everyday use.
